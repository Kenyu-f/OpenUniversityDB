# Research and Other Opportunities

Undergraduate research is a central part of the Caltech undergraduate experience. Caltech states that more than 90% of undergraduates do student research and about half participate during their first year. Programs include SURF, JPL/NASA opportunities, LIGO SURF, and other institute research programs. QuantumSURF invites Caltech and students from other universities to conduct 10 weeks of experimental quantum science and technology research with Caltech groups.
