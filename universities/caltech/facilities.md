# Student Facilities

The Student Activities Center provides 24-hour student-use space, including club rooms, publication work space, music rehearsal space, a small library, a shop, and a movie screening room. Recognized student clubs may use Institute facilities for meetings and events. The Caltech Y also provides low-cost audiovisual and camping-equipment rentals.
