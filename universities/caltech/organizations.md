# Student Organizations

The official 2025–2026 directory includes academic, technical, cultural, religious, recreational, and professional organizations. Recognized clubs can recruit members, advertise events, secure funding, conduct officer elections, provide training, and use Institute facilities. A new club can be registered through the Student and Family Engagement process; the 2026–2027 registration process is open as of September 2026.
