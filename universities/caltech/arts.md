# Arts and Culture

Caltech provides opportunities in music, theatre, visual arts, dance, cinema, photography, and related student organizations. The Performing and Visual Arts department offers classes and performance opportunities. Current clubs include Ballet Club, Ballroom and Swing Dance, Cinema Club, Dance Club, Film Photography Club, Strings Club, and A Cappella.
