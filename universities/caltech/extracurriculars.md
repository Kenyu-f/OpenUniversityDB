# Extracurricular Activities

Caltech reports more than 100 official student-led clubs and organizations. Current examples include Caltech AI Alignment, Caltech Quantum Computing Club, Caltech Math Meet Club, Math Club, Competitive Programming, Undergraduate Computer Science Club, Robotics Team, Physics Club, Hacktech, Quantitative Finance Club, iGEM, Science Olympiad Planning Team, and Data Science Organization. Non-STEM options include music, dance, film, photography, cultural organizations, games, and sports.
