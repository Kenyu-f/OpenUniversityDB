# Athletics and Recreation

Current clubs include Alpine Club, Badminton, Basketball, Climbing, Co-Ed Volleyball, Cricket, Golf, Rugby, Soccer, Squash, Surf and Windsurf, Table Tennis, Tai Chi, Tennis, Triathlon, Ski and Snowboard, and Ultimate Frisbee. The admissions site also identifies students as athletes alongside other extracurricular identities.
