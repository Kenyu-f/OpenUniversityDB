# Student Life

Caltech has a small residential undergraduate environment with substantial student participation in clubs, student government, publications, arts, athletics, and community activities. The Student Activities Center provides space for student government, publications, music rehearsals, club meetings, a small library, a shop, and a movie screening room; most areas are open 24 hours. Caltech's Honor Code applies to academic and extracurricular life. The Caltech Y provides service, activism, outdoor, social, and cultural programs.
