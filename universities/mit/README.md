# MIT — Student Life and Opportunities

MIT has hundreds of student organizations spanning arts, sports, technology, service, games, religion, and cultural activities. MIT Engage is the central portal for organization information. UROP is available across MIT departments, centers, and labs and can be paid or for credit.
