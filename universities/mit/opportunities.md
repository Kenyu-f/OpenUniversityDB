# Research and Other Opportunities

UROP allows undergraduates to collaborate on real research in every MIT department as well as centers and labs. Projects may be paid or for credit and may last a semester, summer, or longer. SuperUROP is a year-long advanced research program for juniors and seniors; the 2026–2027 program includes project proposals, funding decisions, and a two-semester research course.
